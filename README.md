# Quality of Life Assessment Dashboard
### EQ-5D-5L | Personal Perception | Life Goals

An interactive web-based Quality of Life Assessment tool integrating health status, personal perceptions, and individual life goals to provide a broader understanding of wellbeing.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Health is more than the absence of disease. Quality of life is influenced by physical health, emotional wellbeing, personal circumstances, and individual aspirations.

This project introduces a people-centred assessment model combining:

1. **Health Status** – EQ-5D-5L assessment.
2. **Personal Perception** – Positive and negative perceptions of wellbeing.
3. **Life Goals** – Individual priorities and aspirations.

The dashboard generates an individual assessment profile based on user responses.

## ✨ Key Features

- Interactive EQ-5D-5L health assessment.
- Assessment across five health dimensions:
  - Mobility
  - Self-care
  - Usual activities
  - Pain / discomfort
  - Anxiety / depression
- EQ Visual Analogue Scale (VAS).
- Positive and negative perception scoring.
- Personal life goal selection.
- Automated health utility calculation.
- Dynamic individual interpretation.
- Responsive desktop and mobile interface.
- Reset assessment functionality.

---

## 🏥 Public Health Significance

This project explores a broader approach to understanding quality of life beyond conventional clinical health indicators.

By integrating physical health, personal perceptions, and individual aspirations, the model demonstrates how people-centred assessment can contribute to a more holistic understanding of wellbeing.

**Core concept:** Health Status + Personal Perception + Life Goals = Broader Quality of Life Assessment.
